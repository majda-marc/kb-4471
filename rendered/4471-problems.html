parser: dates are read month first

The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose locale writes the day first gets flipped dates: a booking for 7 April arrives as 4 July. This is the problem #4471 was written for, and its fix is the one already queued.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the wrong order

Booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant type what it says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, so the date flips. A parser fix does not reach this: the form’s hint is wrong, not the parser. Only reports that came through the form, at a tenant that runs booking-v3, belong here.

one-day: dates land one day off

These reports describe a date that moved by one day. A flipped month cannot do that. The desk read them as one-day moves (desk-notes.json), and the kb files them under parser only because of the tenant’s locale. The cause is not confirmed. One report puts a client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto on different days for the same meeting, which points at time zones. Engineering has to reproduce it first.

How the reports were counted

  • Each report is counted once, by id.
  • The problem comes from the tenant’s locale and booking form, as in causes.ts.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, so it is not used.

Why one ticket held them all

The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). The same subjects, such as “Wrong day on the invite”, come in for every problem above, so one rule put all of them into one ticket.
